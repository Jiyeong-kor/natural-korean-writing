## Korean document and PDF layout

First distinguish filling a supplied form from authorized free layout editing. A request to enter content in an existing template does not authorize redesigning it. Preserve its tables, rows, columns, merged cells, field positions, labels, instructions, margins, spacing, and established formatting; enter only the verified, applicable values in their designated cells. Check required entries, including verified education, against the source and field list rather than omitting them to simplify the page. Keep an unknown value distinct from a confirmed non-applicable item and resolve required unknowns through authorized sources or a focused question.

For a non-applicable item, preserve the original row, label, notation, and formatting and leave its entry area blank by default. Do not delete, merge, repurpose, or fill it with an invented value or an unsolicited dash or `해당 없음`. If the official form instructions require a particular non-applicable marker, enter that marker in the designated place; do not extend that exception to other fields.

If verified content overflows a fixed cell, do not silently resize the table, move fields, change margins, shrink the font, or drop required facts or approved wording. Use an already permitted entry method or a meaning-preserving shortening only within the granted editing scope. If it still does not fit, identify the affected field and the needed decision instead of changing the template without authority. Apply the following typesetting guidance within that scope.

Treat wording and typesetting as separate tasks. Use a renderer with Korean/CJK line-breaking support and apply the user's stated preference for the actual document. When character-level wrapping is requested, allow automatic line breaks within Korean spacing units rather than retaining each whole unit with a keep-all setting that leaves large line-end gaps. This preference does not authorize imposing that style on a different user or overriding an explicit whole-unit layout request.

For authorized layout editing, change the layout settings, not the approved sentences or paragraph boundaries. Do not insert arbitrary manual newlines or spaces into the manuscript to imitate wrapping. Check line-breaking prohibitions separately, including closing punctuation stranded at the start of a line and opening punctuation stranded at its end. Avoid unnecessary fragmentation of Latin identifiers and URLs; use appropriate supported break opportunities when a long literal cannot fit, without changing its characters. Do not compensate for poor wrapping by excessively stretching character or word spacing to force justified lines.

Inspect the actual rendered pages with the document's final fonts, widths, and output format. Check Korean wrapping, punctuation at line boundaries, Latin literals, line-end gaps, and spacing across the affected pages. After saving or exporting, reopen the saved output and repeat the relevant visual checks; a source setting or a good editor preview does not establish the saved PDF's layout. Compare the saved text with the approved manuscript, distinguishing automatic visual wraps from content changes. Report which output and pages were actually checked and any inaccessible region; if only a plan or preview is available, keep the saved-file check pending rather than claiming completion.

For a filled template, compare the exact final deliverable with both the original form and the verified field values. Check required entries for omissions and wrong-cell placement, non-applicable areas for the required blank or official marker, and unchanged structure and formatting across the document. Inspect the saved pages for clipped or overlapping text, displaced tables, and unintended page breaks as well as readable content. Text extraction alone does not establish layout preservation. Repair discrepancies within the authorized scope and repeat the affected checks before reporting completion; identify any unchecked output or page.
