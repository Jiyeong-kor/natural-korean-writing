# Editing and Review Contract

Read this reference for revision or review, explicit wording or name-quotation constraints, exact-text changes, and required independent review. Apply the entrypoint and mandatory context and punctuation references. These checks do not authorize external disclosure, file operations, or submission.

## Keep a local edit local

Use the user's latest text as the baseline. Distinguish what must be inspected from what may be changed. A request to reread the whole document or return the whole text does not enlarge a local edit.

Preserve wording, order, paragraph boundaries, spaces, and punctuation outside the allowed spans, including text not separately marked approved. A later explicit exception unlocks only that span. A reviewer's suggestion is not the user's permission to change protected or out-of-scope text.

Inspect the affected paragraph and necessary surrounding passages for meaning and duplication. Compare the actual final diff, including reviewer changes: every insertion, deletion, substitution, or movement must be authorized. If a material issue lies outside scope, complete the supported local edit and report the issue separately without silently fixing it. Do not delay an authorized correction for optional stylistic preferences.

Before deleting or softening, check for a lost central claim, supported competence, qualification, reason, transition, or commitment. Remove only the unneeded part of a sentence that also carries necessary content. After successive edits, return the complete affected field or full manuscript requested, not fragments the user must assemble.

## Apply expression restrictions within their scope

Record the target, allowed changes, protected spans, and latest exceptions. An exact-string ban requires checking the whole stated editable scope for that substring, including inside longer words. When the request covers inflected forms or sentence-ending patterns, check those too; a dictionary-form search alone is insufficient. Do not evade a ban by changing spacing or substituting an equally empty formula.

A plausible use is not an exception to a stated ban, but do not extend it to unbanned words, other artifacts, or protected literals. For example, banning one inflected expression does not automatically ban its noun. A quoted test fixture or rule explanation is separate from a manuscript unless explicitly included in the ban.

If the user explicitly bans a string in the entire response while requiring an unchanged literal containing it, the requirements cannot both be satisfied. Identify the exact scope conflict and ask which condition may change. Do not silently rewrite code, remove the required literal, or pretend both checks passed. A later explicit permission to alter a locked occurrence applies only there.

After a word or ending change, preserve the original action and modality. Ability, expectation, intention, promise, and completed work are not interchangeable. Check the assembled text, including any reviewer rewrite, against all still-applicable corrections.

## Quote names by textual role

For each designated name, distinguish body prose, a standalone title or label, a table value, an exact search string, and an actual quotation. Apply the requested marking only to its specified roles. A body-name instruction does not automatically cover titles, tables, other proper nouns, or protected quotations. A title can distinguish a name structurally; follow its requested format rather than treating all title quotation as wrong.

Where quoting applies, preserve the spelling and requested characters, cover later mentions unless first-mention-only was specified, and keep particles outside. Normalize the complete quoted span once; do not blindly wrap a name inside existing marks. With the fictional name `빛지도`, `'빛지도에서'` becomes `'빛지도'에서`, not nested or leftover quotation marks. Inspect adjacent characters and the actual final text for doubled or unmatched marks.

Choosing ASCII rather than curly quotes determines their form where quotation is required; it does not require quotation everywhere. Compare consistency among occurrences with the same role, and compare protected searches and source quotations exactly.

## Check meaning, Korean, and coverage separately

First compare the revision with the supplied facts and latest direct corrections. A user's original account takes precedence over an assistant's earlier interpretation. Keep unknown information unknown; do not invent a favorable or cautious story.

Then read the exact assembled Korean without relying on an English gloss or private explanation. Check predicates, objects, particles, actor changes, collocations, register, and paragraph connections. When an awkward phrase is flagged, repair the relationship in its paragraph rather than swapping a word while keeping the same faulty story. Use the language principles for the relevant diagnosis.

For a personal or application narrative with several examples, make a separate first-paragraph check. Read only its opening one or two sentences and compare the direction they establish with the answer's actual central point. A generic value that could lead to a different story is insufficient. If the intended direction becomes apparent only after unrelated-looking examples, revise the opening or their order within the permitted scope, then reread the entire paragraph and its next transition. Check whether abstract continuity wording names a real source-supported wish, action, or relation instead of merely making the sentences sound connected. Do not count factual accuracy, a checklist pass, or the reviewer's later explanation as a substitute for this language check; do not impose a conclusion-first structure on every genre.

For technical prose, distinguish what a person sees, what is stored, and what the program does with a value. Observation of one state does not establish persistence, a mechanism, or a fix. Keep a common state verb when it is already precise.

For an application, specification, or other reviewed document, keep one concrete language finding and its resolution tied to the outgoing version, or record the passage and distinction checked if no change was needed. Keep this working note out of reader-facing copy. A checklist or PASS label is not evidence of a natural final sentence.

For a requested full audit, inspect every heading, paragraph, table cell, caption, and visible image label in context. Keep a private coverage record and identify inaccessible regions. Keyword search locates candidates; it does not establish complete coverage. The context companion's reader-order pass is still subject to the permitted edit scope.

## Independent review and final version

Use the [agent workflow](agent-workflow.md) for default delegation of substantive application or manuscript work, role boundaries, and restoring an existing task. Honor an applicable established requirement for independent review without asking the user to repeat it. When a separate reviewer is authorized and available, provide the exact question and guidance, source facts, latest corrections, protected spans, allowed edit scope, complete draft, and relevant length/counting rule. Identify the skill revision to read. Require the full core and applicable reference readings.

Prefer a fresh reviewer without the writer's reasoning or preferred verdict. Ask for findings against the supplied material, not endorsement of an explanation. Observe the user's required review channel and scope. Internal delegation stays within the authorized task and the current host's actual capabilities; this procedure grants no permission to share private material with another service or create unrelated external tasks. Repository benchmark reviewers instead follow their separate evaluation protocol and must not receive these skill instructions.

Reconcile substantive findings with the source. A reviewer cannot unlock wording, invent evidence, or expand the assignment. Keep actual returned findings distinct from pending requests, unavailable review, and self-review. After accepted changes, the writer and responsible reviewer compare the whole actual diff with the baseline and reread the assembled result within their required scope. The coordinator verifies the actual returned findings and identity of the delivered version; coordinating alone is not a claim of substantive review. If instructions or text changed, update affected reviewers and verify the returned work applies to that version; sending an update is not completion.

Resolve material errors or explicitly bound missing evidence before calling the review finished. Optional preferences do not require endless rewriting. If saving, posting, or form entry is separately authorized, read back the accessible saved text and compare it with the canonical version, including carried-over text and required spellings. A correct draft or save acknowledgement is not proof of correct persistence. State any unchecked destination or region.

Use the application workflow for whole-answer chat delivery, exact question text and character counts, same-company consistency, approval stages, and form operations. Do not apply those output formats to every short reply or local wording suggestion.
